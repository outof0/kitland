# Kitland Brand Guidelines

**Domain:** kitland.dev  
**Brand idea:** The developer workbench.  
**Tagline:** Tools out. Work on.  
**Descriptor:** Everyday developer tools, in one place.

## 1. Positioning

Kitland should feel **practical, fast, friendly, systematic**. The identity is designed for a developer product, not a generic SaaS template.

## 2. Naming & casing

Use **Kitland** in all user-facing copy. Use `kitland` for URLs, repositories, package names and technical identifiers. Never invent alternate casing.

## 3. Logo system

- `lockup-primary.svg`: default light-background lockup.
- `lockup-reverse.svg`: use on dark/brand backgrounds.
- `symbol-primary.svg`: standalone mark for compact product surfaces.
- `symbol-small.svg`: optically adjusted small-size master for 16–32 px contexts.
- `wordmark.svg`: outlined wordmark; do not typeset a replacement.

### Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to at least **25% of the symbol width** on every side.

### Minimum size

- Full lockup: 96 px wide digital minimum.
- Primary symbol: 24 px digital minimum.
- Below 24 px use the supplied `symbol-small.svg`/favicon master.

### Do not

- Stretch, skew, rotate or redraw the mark.
- Add gradients, shadows or outlines to the primary logo.
- Split the wordmark into multiple colors or weights.
- Put `.dev` inside the standard logo.

## 4. Color

Primary brand color: `#2563EB`. Brand color communicates identity; semantic colors communicate state. Do not substitute one role for another.

## 5. Typography

- UI/body: **Inter**
- Display/headlines: **Manrope**
- Logo wordmark is artwork and is independent of product typography.
- Use sentence case for product UI, headings and buttons.

Fonts are **not bundled** in this kit. Add them through your normal licensed/open-source font delivery pipeline.

## 6. Iconography

Use simple line icons with consistent optical size. Product icons should inherit text/semantic color unless an icon is explicitly a branded surface. Avoid decorative multicolor icon sets.

## 7. Accessibility

Do not rely on color alone for status. Pair success/warning/error with label, icon or other affordance. Verify actual UI foreground/background pairs against WCAG during implementation; palette membership does not guarantee contrast in every combination.

## 8. Product principle

**The developer workbench.**

Personality: Practical · Fast · Friendly · Systematic.
