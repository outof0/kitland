## Outcome

One authoritative, machine-readable list of the 64 intended tools, including
family, UI pattern, platform contract, core contract, design state, and risk
tier. Landing, navigation, work assignment, and the product release gate derive
from this inventory.

## Blocker

The repository states a 64-tool target but does not contain the complete list.
The product owner must provide or identify that source of truth; implementers
must not substitute a guessed generic-tools list.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Exactly 64 stable unique ids and slugs.
- [ ] Every tool belongs to one supported family and UI pattern.
- [ ] Required platforms and capabilities are explicit.
- [ ] Design-ready/design-needed and risk tier are explicit.
- [ ] Catalog tests reject missing, duplicate, or undeclared entries.
- [ ] Release gate reports progress and blocks publication until all entries are ready.

## Security, privacy, and accessibility

Capabilities that imply network, filesystem, credentials, cryptography, or
large payloads require an explicit review flag before assignment.
