# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Kitland to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or preferred tools.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code in good faith.
- Give actionable feedback about work, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy; never post credentials, private keys, personal data, or user
  payloads in issues, pull requests, or screenshots.
- Assume mistakes can be corrected and help make corrections possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello.outof0@gmail.com` with the subject
`[Kitland conduct]`. Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Project
maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or limit participation to
protect the community.
